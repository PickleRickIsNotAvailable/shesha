Questions

Do I need data or Wi-Fi?

Only once to open the page. After that Shesha works completely offline, even in airplane mode. Sending a WhatsApp receipt or reminder uses WhatsApp as normal.

Does it work on my cheap phone?

Yes. Any Android phone with Chrome from the last 6 years, and iPhones too. It's smaller than a single photo.

What if I lose my phone?

Use Shop → Backup to send your data file to yourself on WhatsApp or email. On a new phone, tap Restore.

Who can see my sales?

Only you. Nothing leaves your phone unless you send it yourself.

Can I try it first?

The app opens with a demo shop already full of sales so you can play with it. When you're ready, tap Shop → Start fresh.
